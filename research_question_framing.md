# Research Question and Provisional Lane

## Provisional Lane
Google Search Ranking & Discoverability

## Research Question
Can machine learning identify webpages that are most likely to experience declining search performance so content teams can prioritize which pages should be refreshed first?

## Unit of Analysis
Individual webpage.

## Output
A ranked list of webpages with a predicted likelihood of decline and a priority score for content refresh.

## Decision
Which webpages should be refreshed first.

## Action
Content managers review the highest-ranked pages, update their content, improve SEO, and monitor search performance after publishing the changes.

## Cost of a Wrong Recommendation
If the model incorrectly flags pages, teams may waste time updating pages that do not need changes. If it misses declining pages, valuable traffic and search visibility may continue to decrease.

## Why Machine Learning?
This problem involves many interacting signals such as impressions, CTR, content age, search position, and update history. Machine learning can recognize patterns across these features better than simple hand-written rules, helping prioritize content more effectively while still requiring human review.
